# Contributing Guidelines

## Workflow

1. Review [README.md](README.md), [NAVIGATION.md](NAVIGATION.md), and the relevant research guidance.
2. Create a branch for the work.
3. Add research or edits to the appropriate folder.
4. Include sources, citations, authorship, and dates.
5. Apply [DATA_CLASSIFICATION.md](DATA_CLASSIFICATION.md).
6. For any example, dataset, case material, quotation, screenshot, media, export, or derived output, follow [SANITISATION_GUIDE.md](SANITISATION_GUIDE.md) and complete [PUBLICATION_CHECKLIST.md](PUBLICATION_CHECKLIST.md).
7. Submit a pull request for human review.
8. Record an approved public-release decision in [PUBLICATION_LOG.md](PUBLICATION_LOG.md).

Do not include personal data, confidential source locations, credentials, restricted redaction details, or non-public approval evidence in a branch, pull request, issue, checklist, or public log.

## Documentation Standards

Each research document should identify:

- author
- date
- sources
- summary
- major revision history

Use credible academic, government, standards-body, NGO, technology, and accessibility sources. Avoid unsourced claims and reliance on promotional material alone.

## Authority Boundary

Passing validation or receiving code-review approval does not establish consent, publication authority, accessibility approval, legal compliance, or permission to reuse protected material. Missing or uncertain permission is a stop condition.
