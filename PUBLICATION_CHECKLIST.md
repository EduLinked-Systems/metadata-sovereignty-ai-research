## Classification and Origin

- [ ] The artefact is classified under [DATA_CLASSIFICATION.md](DATA_CLASSIFICATION.md).
- Classification: `PUBLIC` / `INTERNAL` / `SENSITIVE_OR_RESTRICTED`
- Origin: `SYNTHETIC` / `PUBLIC_SOURCE` / `NON_PUBLIC_SOURCE`
- [ ] Only material classified `PUBLIC` is proposed for release.
- [ ] If synthetic, the artefact is not derived from a real person or case.
- [ ] If public-source material is reused, sources, attribution, and reuse rights are recorded.
- [ ] If non-public source material informed the artefact, the sanitisation decision is documented outside the public repository at an authorised location.

## Identity and Sensitive Content

- [ ] Direct identifiers are absent.
- [ ] Dates, locations, organisations, rare events, quotations, and combined details have been checked for reidentification risk.
- [ ] Filenames, URLs, document properties, comments, tracked changes, hidden content, linked files, and media metadata have been checked.
- [ ] The artefact contains no real case record, evidence register, interview, incident detail, chain-of-custody material, or restricted legal, forensic, clinical, medical, or safeguarding note.
- [ ] Internal system, repository, Drive, SharePoint, credential, and access details are absent.

## Authority, Rights, and Meaning

- [ ] Consent or another valid publication authority covers this artefact, purpose, format, and destination.
- [ ] Authorship, attribution, licence, copyright, cultural authority, and community authority have been checked.
- [ ] AI processing and transformation permissions have been checked.
- [ ] Sanitisation or transformation does not misrepresent authorship, consent, evidence, or community meaning.
- [ ] Relevant accessible formats have received human accessibility and modality review.
- [ ] Any publication conditions or expiry/withdrawal requirements are recorded.
