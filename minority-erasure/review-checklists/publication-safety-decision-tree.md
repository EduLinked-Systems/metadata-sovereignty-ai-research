# Publication Safety Decision Tree

This decision tree is for public release review of minority-authored, lived-experience, accessibility, advocacy, or community-governance material.

## Step 1: Is This Real?

If the material is about a real person, real group, real client, real participant, or real incident, require consent and authority review.

If the material is synthetic, label it clearly as synthetic and continue.

## Step 2: Is Authorship Preserved?

If authorship is missing, unknown, replaced, or weakened, stop and escalate.

If authorship is protected but not public, confirm protected metadata exists before publication.

## Step 3: Is Consent Clear?

If consent is missing, expired, withdrawn, contradictory, or out of scope, treat the record as not publishable.

If consent is public, specific, current, and within scope, continue.

## Step 4: Are Access Needs Preserved?

If required formats are missing from the publication plan, mark accessibility erasure risk and require review.

If required formats are included, continue.

## Step 5: Did AI Touch The Material?

If AI generated, summarised, classified, translated, or reformatted the material, require disclosure and human review.

If AI changed meaning, attribution, authority, uncertainty, or tone, stop and revise.

## Step 6: Can It Be Public?

| Review Result | Publication Status |
| --- | --- |
| Synthetic, labelled, and structurally valid | Public example allowed. |
| Real but consent unclear | Not publishable. |
| Real and consented but human review incomplete | Public release paused. |
| Accessibility review incomplete | Public draft only. |
| AI changed meaning | Not publishable until corrected and reviewed. |
