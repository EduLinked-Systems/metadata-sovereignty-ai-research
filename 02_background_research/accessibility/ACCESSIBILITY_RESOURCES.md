## Research-use rules

- Preserve source title, author/community authority and provenance.
- Distinguish research evidence from current implementation authority.
- Treat Easy Read, AAC/symbol support, signed language, translation/localisation and participant communication preference as separate but interoperable concerns.
- Do not infer participant preference merely because a modality is available.
- Do not convert an automated check, generated derivative or publication citation into accessibility-conformance evidence.
- Bind real accessibility transformation/verification evidence through the existing Core lifecycle rather than creating a repository-local evidence store.
