# Publication Log

This log records public-release decisions without exposing confidential source material. It is an approval index, not a redaction log or evidence store.

Do not record personal data, restricted source locations, credentials, private-system identifiers, or sensitive redaction details here. Store any necessary protected evidence only in an authorised non-public system and cite a privacy-safe decision reference.

No approval is inferred for material published before this log was adopted. Historical entries require attributable evidence and must not be reconstructed from assumption.

| _YYYY-MM-DD_ | _path or identifier_ | _version_ | _SYNTHETIC / PUBLIC_SOURCE / SANITISED_ | _exact public destination_ | _APPROVE / APPROVE_WITH_CONDITIONS / WITHDRAWN_ | _role_ | _privacy-safe reference_ | _none or concise condition_ |

## Entry Rules

- Add one row for each exact artefact/version/destination decision.
- Use `SANITISED` only after completing [SANITISATION_GUIDE.md](SANITISATION_GUIDE.md) and [PUBLICATION_CHECKLIST.md](PUBLICATION_CHECKLIST.md).
- Record a new row when a material revision, format, destination, consent state, licence, or decision changes.
- Mark withdrawals without adding sensitive reasons to this public file.
- A log entry records an attributable decision; it does not transfer source authority or approve other derivatives or destinations.
